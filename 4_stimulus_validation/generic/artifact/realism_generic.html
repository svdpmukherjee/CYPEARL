How realistic the generic emails read

Sixteen everyday emails, the ones that arrive because you work at the company rather than because of the job you do, rated 1–10 for realism by – people after reading each one. The eight conditions are the same factorial as the job-specific set: sender standing × urgency × framing, two emails in each. The pooled panel shows the whole distribution behind each condition; every other number on the page is a mean with ±1 SE.
